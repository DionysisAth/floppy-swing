# Floppy Swing privacy policy

_Last updated: [date]. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing._

Floppy Swing ("the game") is made by [developer name]. This policy explains
what the game collects and why.

## Without an internet connection

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, items, settings and best-run
ghosts) is stored only on your device.

## Google Play Games and Game Center

The game has no server or accounts of its own. If you sign in to **Google Play
Games** (Android) or **Game Center** (iOS), the game uses them to:

- post your **Daily Challenge times and Endless scores** to leaderboards,
  shown with your Play Games / Game Center name;
- unlock **achievements**;
- keep a **cloud copy of your game progress** in your Play Games / iCloud
  account, so a new phone picks up where you left off.

That data is held by Google or Apple under their policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
We never see your email or contact details, and we don't sell any data.

## Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers
and usage data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for
example in the EU/UK) the game asks for your consent first, and on iOS it
asks for permission before tracking. You can change your choice at any time
in Settings > Privacy options. See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs, the Starter Pack, No Ads, the Premium
Pass) are handled entirely by **Google Play** or the **App Store**. We never
see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation and a
transaction number from the store, which it keeps on your device (and in your
cloud save) so a purchase is never paid out twice and can be restored.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules **Daily Challenge reminders**
on your device (at most one a day, for the next few days). They are created
on the device; no server sends them and nothing is collected. Turn them off in
Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

## Deleting your data

Settings > Reset progress wipes the progress on your device, and uninstalling
the game removes it. Your leaderboard scores, achievements and cloud save
live in your Google Play Games or Game Center account: remove them there
(Play Games app > Settings > Delete Play Games account and data, or on iOS
Settings > Game Center). You can also email [contact email] with questions.

## Children

The game is intended for players aged [12] and over. It does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Security and retention

The game stores nothing on servers of its own. Google and Apple keep
leaderboard, achievement and saved-game data under their own retention
policies.

## Changes and contact

We'll post changes to this policy here. Questions: [contact email].
